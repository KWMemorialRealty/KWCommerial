Houston commercial real estate market, Q3 2026

Houston's industrial and retail markets stayed tight in Q3 2026, with record industrial rents and retail vacancy below 6% for five straight years. Office vacancy remained high overall, but demand is concentrating in newer buildings.

Office

Overall vacancy edged up to 24.6% as consolidations and move-outs offset steady leasing. Year-to-date leasing is up 5.7%, and asking rents are the highest since 2021, a sign that demand is splitting toward newer, amenity-rich buildings.

Industrial

The strongest quarter of net absorption since 2022 kept vacancy at 6.3% despite heavy new supply. The construction pipeline is easing, down to 19.6 million sq ft, and average asking rents reached a record $8.16 per sq ft.

Retail

Vacancy has stayed below 6% for 20 consecutive quarters. New deliveries are down 22% from last year, which keeps space scarce, and asking rents rose 8.2% over the year, the strongest annual growth on record.

What it means

For owners, tenants and investors.

Tenants

Office tenants have room to negotiate, especially in older buildings. Industrial and retail tenants face tighter markets, so starting a search early matters.

Owners and landlords

Industrial and retail owners are seeing rising rents. Office owners compete for a smaller pool of tenants, where building quality and concessions decide the deal.

Investors

Steady job growth supports demand across property types. Every property is different, so we pair these market figures with a property-level analysis.

Questions

Common questions.

What is the office vacancy rate in Houston?

Houston's overall office vacancy was 24.6% in Q3 2026, according to Cushman & Wakefield, with negative 290,000 sq ft of net absorption for the quarter. Leasing activity was still up 5.7% year to date, and newer, high-quality buildings are performing much better than older ones.

How is the Houston industrial market doing?

Houston industrial vacancy held at 6.3% in Q3 2026, with 7.3 million sq ft of net absorption, the strongest quarter since 2022, and record average asking rents of $8.16 per sq ft (Cushman & Wakefield).

Is Houston retail space hard to find?

Retail vacancy was 5.7% in Q3 2026 and has stayed below 6% for 20 straight quarters, with asking rents up 8.2% over the year (Cushman & Wakefield). Well-located space, especially necessity-based retail, tends to lease quickly.

How often is this page updated?

The market figures are updated each quarter after the major brokerage research reports are released. The job and unemployment charts update automatically from the Federal Reserve's FRED database as new official data comes out.

What to evaluate

Before you buy, sell or lease.

  • Environmental history and a Phase I assessment
  • Oil and water separators and drainage
  • Water and sewer capacity, and water reclamation for car washes

Questions

Common questions.

Do you sell car washes in Houston?

Yes. We help owners sell car washes, whether the business, the real estate or both, and help operators and investors find them.

How is a car wash valued?

Buyers look at cars washed, revenue per car, membership income, operating costs, equipment condition and the real estate.

What is an express tunnel car wash?

A car wash where vehicles move on a conveyor through an automated tunnel, usually with free vacuums and monthly memberships. Many new car washes are built this way.

Why do auto properties need environmental checks?

Uses such as repair shops, quick-lube and car washes handle oil, fuel and chemicals, so buyers and lenders usually require a Phase I environmental site assessment, which can lead to further testing.

What do I need to open a used car lot in Texas?

A dealer license from the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles, which has requirements for the business location. We check a property against those requirements before you sign a lease.

Can I lease an auto repair building?

Yes. Many shops lease. Key points include bay count, door height, lifts, power, parking for customer cars and whether the lease allows automotive use.

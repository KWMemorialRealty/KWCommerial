Questions

Common questions.

What commercial property types does KW Commercial Texas handle?

Retail, office, industrial, multifamily, land and development, medical and hospitality property, plus special-purpose property such as churches, gas stations, self-storage, restaurants, car washes and childcare centers, and investment property and business sales, across greater Houston and Texas.

How do I find an agent for my property type?

Call 713.470.2100 or email kwmcommercialtx@gmail.com and we will connect you with an agent who works on your property type. You can also browse our full agent roster.

Do you handle special-purpose property such as churches or gas stations?

Yes. Our agents work on churches, gas stations and convenience stores, self-storage, restaurants, car washes and auto property, and childcare and school property, which are often operating businesses as well as real estate.

Do you represent tenants as well as owners?

Yes. We represent businesses leasing space through tenant representation, and owners leasing or selling their property.

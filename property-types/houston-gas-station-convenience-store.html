Questions

Common questions.

Do you sell gas stations and convenience stores in Houston?

Yes. We help owners sell stations, convenience stores and truck stops, whether that is the business, the real estate or both, and help buyers and investors find them.

How are gas stations valued?

Buyers look at fuel volume and margin, inside sales, other income, the condition of the tanks and equipment, and whether the real estate is included. Value is often a combination of the business and the property.

What environmental checks does a gas station need?

Buyers and lenders usually require a Phase I environmental site assessment, which can lead to further testing. In Texas, underground storage tanks are regulated by the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ), so tank records and compliance are reviewed closely.

What is a fuel supply agreement?

It is a contract with a fuel distributor or brand that sets supply, branding and pricing terms for a period of years. It may bind a buyer or require a buyout, so it is reviewed early in a sale.

Can I buy the business and lease the property?

Yes. Some buyers purchase the business and lease the site, others buy both. Each option changes the price, financing and long-term costs.

Do licenses and permits transfer to a new owner?

No. Permits for beer and wine (TABC), lottery and tobacco sales are issued to the owner, so the new owner applies in its own name, and that timing is built into the sale.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you help restaurants find space in Houston?

Yes. We help independent owners, franchisees and restaurant groups find space to lease or buy, and help landlords lease restaurant space.

What is second-generation restaurant space?

Space that was a restaurant before and still has some of its kitchen in place, such as the hood and grease trap. It can save significant build-out cost and time, if the layout and equipment suit the new concept.

What should I look for in a restaurant lease?

Common points include the tenant improvement allowance, free rent during build-out, use and exclusive clauses, patio and signage rights, hours, and what happens to the equipment when the lease ends.

Can any restaurant space serve alcohol?

Not always. Permits come from the Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission (TABC), and local rules can limit sales near schools, churches and hospitals. We check this before you sign a lease.

What is a restaurant pad site?

A separate parcel, usually at the edge of a shopping center parking lot, for a freestanding building, often with a drive-thru. Pads can be leased as land, built for a tenant, or sold.

What is a net-leased restaurant?

A building leased to a restaurant operator who pays rent plus most property expenses. Investors buy them for steady income, and value depends on the lease term and the operator's strength.

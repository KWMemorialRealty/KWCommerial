Questions

Common questions.

How is hotel performance measured?

Hotels are commonly measured by occupancy, average daily rate (ADR) and revenue per available room (RevPAR), which is room revenue divided by the number of available rooms.

How are hotels valued?

Like other income property, hotels are usually valued on net operating income, with added attention to brand, management and the property's operating history.

What is a property improvement plan (PIP)?

A PIP is a list of renovations a hotel brand requires, often when a hotel is sold or its franchise is renewed. Its cost can significantly affect a hotel's price.

What is the difference between a branded and an independent hotel?

A branded hotel operates under a franchise such as a national chain and follows its standards and reservation system. An independent hotel has its own brand and more control, without franchise fees.

Is a hotel sale different from other commercial sales?

Yes. A hotel is both real estate and an operating business, so buyers review operating results, the franchise agreement, staff and furniture and equipment as well as the property.

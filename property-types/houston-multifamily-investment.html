From first call to closing.

Questions

Common questions.

How are multifamily properties valued?

Multifamily property is usually valued on its income. Net operating income (rent and other income minus operating expenses) divided by the market capitalization rate gives an estimate of value.

What is a cap rate?

The capitalization rate is a property's net operating income divided by its price. It lets investors compare the yield of different properties.

What is a rent roll?

A rent roll lists each unit with its tenant, rent, lease dates and deposits. Buyers use it to verify the income a property actually produces.

What is a value-add multifamily property?

It is a property where an investor expects to raise income through renovations, better management or bringing rents in line with the market.

What is a 1031 exchange?

A 1031 exchange lets an investor defer capital gains taxes by reinvesting sale proceeds into another investment property under IRS rules and deadlines. Tax questions should go to a qualified tax adviser.

How many units make a property commercial multifamily?

Properties with five or more units are generally treated as commercial for financing. Buildings with two to four units are usually financed as residential.

How a deal works

From first call to closing.

  2. Search and tourWe find medical-ready space and space that can be converted, then tour it with you.

Questions

Common questions.

How is medical office space different from regular office space?

Medical space often needs more plumbing, stronger electrical service, specific layouts for exam rooms and accessibility features, so build-out costs and lease terms tend to differ from standard office space.

Should a medical practice lease or buy its space?

Practices that plan to stay long term often consider buying to build equity, while leasing keeps capital free for equipment and growth. A medical property agent can compare both.

Why does medical space cost more to build out?

Medical space typically needs added plumbing, electrical capacity, specialized ventilation and accessibility features, so build-out costs usually exceed those of standard office space.

What is an on-campus versus off-campus medical office building?

On-campus buildings sit on or next to a hospital campus. Off-campus buildings are located in the community, often closer to where patients live.

How long are medical office leases?

Medical leases are often longer than standard office leases, partly because practices invest heavily in their build-out and want to stay in place.

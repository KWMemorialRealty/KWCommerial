Questions

Common questions.

Do you sell self-storage facilities in Houston?

Yes. We help owners sell facilities, help investors and operators buy them, and help developers find land for new storage across greater Houston.

How is a self-storage facility valued?

Like other income property, mostly on net operating income and a capitalization rate. Buyers also look at occupancy, how current rents compare with the market, and room to grow income.

What is the difference between street rate and in-place rent?

Street rate is what a facility charges new customers today. In-place rent is what existing customers pay. A gap between the two can show room to raise income.

Is climate-controlled storage worth more?

Climate-controlled units usually rent for more than standard units, and in Houston's heat and humidity many customers ask for them. Building them also costs more, so the right mix depends on the local market.

Can an existing building be converted to self-storage?

Yes. Former retail, office and industrial buildings are converted to storage when the layout, access, local rules and local demand support it.

What is boat and RV storage?

Covered, enclosed or open parking for boats, RVs and trailers. It needs more land than standard storage and is common near lakes, the coast and suburban neighborhoods.

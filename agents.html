Questions

Common questions.

Do your agents speak languages other than English?

Yes. Among our agents are advisors who work in Arabic, Cantonese, Greek, Italian, Korean, Mandarin, Persian (Farsi), Romanian, Spanish and Vietnamese. Use the language filter above to find them. If your language isn't one of these, our commercial agents can partner with a colleague in our 500+ agent office: the office's International Division includes colleagues who speak more than 40 languages.

Can my commercial agent also help with a home purchase?

Yes. When a business owner, investor or relocating executive also needs a home in Houston, their commercial advisor stays their contact, working alongside the office's luxury and international specialists.

Do you work with international investors?

Yes. Our commercial professionals serve investors buying Houston property from abroad, with access to KW's referral network of more than 165,000 agents across 55+ regions, including 17,600+ outside the U.S., and with advisors who work in languages from Spanish to Mandarin. The network works both ways: KW agents abroad refer their clients to us, and we connect our clients with KW agents in other countries. Tax and legal questions go to qualified professionals.

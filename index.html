Questions

Common questions.

What does KW Commercial Texas do?

KW Commercial Texas is a Houston commercial real estate brokerage. Our commercial professionals represent property owners in sales and leases, help investors acquire property, help businesses find their next location, evaluate development opportunities and prepare market reports for clients.

Why choose KW Commercial Texas?

KW Commercial Texas is Houston's largest KW Commercial office, #1 in volume and agent count. Clients get an advisor held to the broker's standards, with legal and compliance counsel and transaction support behind every deal; a monthly Property Information Exchange where we host brokers from across Houston to share commercial opportunities; video marketing available from our in-house Content Lab; and the reach of the world's largest real estate franchise by agent count.

Can you tell me what my commercial property is worth?

Yes. Our commercial professionals provide pricing and valuation guidance through broker opinions of value: a current estimate of what your property could sell or lease for, based on comparable sales and market conditions. A broker opinion of value is not an appraisal; if you need a formal appraisal, for example for a loan, a state-licensed appraiser provides it.

How does the KW network help my property?

KW Commercial Texas is connected to the world's largest real estate franchise by agent count, with more than 165,000 KW agents across 55+ regions, including 17,600+ outside the U.S., and 1,800+ KW Commercial members across North America. That network brings more buyers, tenants and international investors to your property, and connects you with KW professionals if you are expanding or investing in another market.

Where is KW Commercial Texas located?

Our office is at 1220 Augusta Drive, Suite 300, Houston, TX 77057. We serve clients across greater Houston and Texas. Call 713.470.2100.

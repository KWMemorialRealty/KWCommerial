Questions

Common questions.

Which areas of Houston do you cover?

All of greater Houston. Our area pages cover Katy, the Energy Corridor, Downtown Houston, Sugar Land, The Woodlands and Cypress, and our agents work across the rest of the region and Texas.

Where do the office market figures come from?

From Cushman & Wakefield's Houston office MarketBeat for Q3 2026. We update them each quarter.

Which Houston submarkets have the lowest office vacancy?

In Q3 2026, Katy/Grand Parkway West had 5.8% office vacancy and The Woodlands/Spring had 12.5%, against 24.6% for greater Houston (Cushman & Wakefield).

Do zoning rules differ across Houston-area cities?

Yes. The City of Houston has no zoning ordinance and relies on deed restrictions and development rules, while cities such as Sugar Land and Katy have zoning, and unincorporated areas follow county rules.

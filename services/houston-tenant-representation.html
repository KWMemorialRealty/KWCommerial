Questions

Common questions.

Do you represent commercial tenants in Houston?

Yes. We represent businesses leasing office, retail, industrial and medical space across greater Houston, from first search to signed lease, and on renewals and relocations.

What does a tenant representative do?

A tenant representative works for the tenant, not the landlord. They find and compare space, analyze total costs, negotiate the lease terms, and coordinate with your attorney and contractors through move-in.

Who pays the tenant representative?

In most Houston commercial leases, the landlord pays the tenant representative's fee from the commission it has agreed to pay, so the tenant does not pay directly. Your representation agreement spells out the terms.

When should I start looking for new space?

Many businesses start 12 to 18 months before their lease ends, and earlier for larger or specialized space. That leaves time to compare options, negotiate and build out the space.

Why not just call the landlord's agent?

The landlord's agent represents the landlord. A tenant representative compares the whole market, including other buildings, and negotiates on your side for rent, concessions and lease terms.

Should my business lease or buy?

Buying can build equity and lock in costs for a business that plans to stay; leasing keeps capital free and makes it easier to move or grow. We can compare both for your situation.

What is the Information About Brokerage Services notice?

Texas requires real estate brokers to give this notice early on. It explains the ways a broker can represent you. Our notice is linked at the bottom of every page.

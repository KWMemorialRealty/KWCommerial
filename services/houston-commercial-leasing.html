Questions

Common questions.

Do you lease commercial property for landlords in Houston?

Yes. We represent owners leasing office, retail, industrial and medical space across greater Houston, including lease-up of new buildings and renewals.

How is commercial space marketed for lease?

Through commercial listing platforms, signage, direct outreach to tenant representatives and businesses, and the KW network. Our agents can also use video marketing from our in-house Content Lab.

What is the difference between a triple net and a full-service lease?

In a triple net lease, the tenant pays rent plus its share of taxes, insurance and maintenance. In a full-service lease, those costs are included in the rent. Retail and industrial space is usually triple net, and multi-tenant office is often full service.

How long does it take to lease commercial space?

Property type, location, condition and price drive the timeline. Well-priced, move-in-ready space in a strong location leases faster, which is why pricing and presentation matter.

Should I offer a tenant improvement allowance?

Yes, especially for office and medical space. An allowance can attract stronger tenants and longer leases. We help weigh its cost against the rent and term.

Can you help a landlord retain existing tenants?

Yes. Planning renewals early, before a tenant starts touring other space, is one of the simplest ways to reduce vacancy.

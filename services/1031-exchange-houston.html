1031 exchanges: a guide for Houston investors

A 1031 exchange lets an investor sell investment or business real estate and buy other real estate while deferring capital gains tax. KW Commercial Texas helps investors sell and find replacement property in greater Houston. Tax and legal advice comes from your CPA, attorney and qualified intermediary.

What working with us typically looks like.

A typical 1031 exchange

  1. Plan with your advisorsTalk with your CPA and attorney, and choose a qualified intermediary before the sale.
  2. Sell the propertyThe intermediary holds the proceeds when the sale closes.
  3. Identify replacementsWithin 45 days, identify replacement property in writing.
  4. Buy the replacementClose within 180 days, using the funds held by the intermediary.
  5. Report the exchangeYour tax preparer reports the exchange on your return.

Questions

Common questions.

What is a 1031 exchange?

Named for Section 1031 of the Internal Revenue Code, it lets an investor sell real property held for investment or business use and buy other like-kind real property while deferring capital gains tax.

What are the 1031 exchange deadlines?

You have 45 days from the sale to identify replacement property in writing, and 180 days from the sale, or your tax return due date if earlier, to close on it. The deadlines are strict and are not extended for weekends.

What counts as like-kind property?

For real estate, most investment or business property qualifies for most other real property held the same way. An office building can be exchanged for land, a retail center or rental property. A primary residence does not qualify.

What is a qualified intermediary?

An independent party that holds the sale proceeds and handles the exchange documents. If the seller receives the money directly, the exchange can fail, so the intermediary is arranged before the sale closes.

What is boot in a 1031 exchange?

Cash or other value the investor receives, including debt that is not replaced. Boot is generally taxable, which is why investors aim to buy property of equal or greater value.

How many properties can I identify?

Common rules allow identifying up to three properties of any value, or more if their total value stays within limits set by IRS rules. Your qualified intermediary can explain which rule fits your exchange.

Do you give tax advice on 1031 exchanges?

No. We help you sell and find replacement property. Your CPA, attorney and qualified intermediary advise on the tax and legal side.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you represent commercial tenants in Houston?

Yes. We represent businesses leasing office, retail, industrial and medical space across greater Houston, from the search through lease negotiation and renewals.

Do you lease property for landlords?

Yes. We market vacant space, find and qualify tenants, and negotiate leases for owners of office, retail, industrial and medical property.

Can you help with a 1031 exchange?

Yes. We help investors sell property and find replacement property within the 45-day and 180-day deadlines, alongside their qualified intermediary, CPA and attorney.

Do you help buyers and sellers of commercial property?

Yes. We represent sellers, buyers and investors in sales of every commercial property type. See our property types for details.

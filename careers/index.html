Questions

Common questions.

What kind of work do commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas do?

Commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas work in investment sales, leasing, acquisitions and development across retail, office, industrial, multifamily, land, farm and ranch, healthcare, hospitality and mixed-use property across greater Houston and beyond.

How does the team work with other specialists?

KW Commercial Texas holds PIE, a monthly Property Information Exchange open to brokers from across Houston, and Connect & Strategy, its monthly in-house meeting, and its commercial professionals work alongside the office's Luxury and International Divisions and 500+ agents.

Can a residential agent move into commercial at KW Memorial?

Yes. Agents new to commercial follow KW Commercial's Performance Path (Commercial Bridge, Applied Foundations and APEX Mentorship) and work new property types under a formal mentoring agreement.

Who handles commercial work at KW Commercial Texas?

Commercial work at KW Commercial Texas is reserved for professionals who meet the broker's commercial standards. Agents growing into a new property type follow a clear path, working alongside an experienced mentor under a formal mentoring agreement.

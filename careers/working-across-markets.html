Why it matters

One relationship, many transactions.

The people commercial professionals serve often need more than one thing. Here, you can serve the whole relationship.

The business owner

You lease or sell their building. A year later they're buying a home in River Oaks or Bellaire. You handle both.

The overseas investor

They buy a Houston retail center, then want a residence for their family. You stay their advisor on both sides.

The relocating executive

Their company needs office space; their family needs a home. One trusted contact, working with the right specialists.

The luxury client

Your luxury buyer owns a company that needs a new location. The commercial work stays with someone they already trust.

How it works

Freedom, with the same standards.

The same broker standards, legal support and mentoring apply to every transaction.

  1. One firm, one standard. The broker sets standards for each type of work, with legal and compliance counsel behind every transaction.
  2. Partner with the specialist. On unfamiliar ground, you work alongside a colleague from the Luxury or International Division, or an agent team that knows that market.
  3. Mentoring for new property types. On the commercial side, new property types are handled under a formal mentoring agreement with an experienced commercial professional.
  4. Referrals that travel. KW's referral network connects you with KW agents across the U.S. and around the world when a client's needs go beyond Houston.

Questions

Common questions.

Can commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas also work residential?

Yes. Commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas can also serve their clients' luxury and international residential needs, working alongside the office's Luxury and International Divisions.

Does working residential take focus away from commercial?

No. Commercial work is the core of KW Commercial Texas. Serving a client's other real estate needs keeps the relationship with the person who already knows them, while specialists handle the details.

Who helps with international clients?

The office's International Division, with agents who speak more than 40 languages, works with KW Commercial Texas on clients investing from abroad, and KW's international network supports referrals.

Do the same standards apply?

Yes. At KW Commercial Texas the broker sets standards for each type of work, with legal and compliance counsel behind every transaction.

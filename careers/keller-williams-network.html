Questions

Common questions.

How big is the KW network?

KW is the world’s largest real estate franchise by agent count, with more than 165,000 agents across 55+ regions (KW, 2026), including more than 17,600 outside the U.S. (November 2025). KW Commercial, its commercial division, has more than 1,800 members in North America.

Can commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas receive referrals from other KW agents?

Yes. Commercial professionals at KW Commercial Texas receive referrals from KW agents in Houston and beyond whose clients own businesses, lease space or invest in commercial property.

What does KW Commercial membership include?

KW Commercial membership includes Applied Foundations of Commercial Real Estate training, the RealNex platform, a CCIM Institute partnership with member discounts, product specialty groups with monthly masterminds and a member network on Circle.

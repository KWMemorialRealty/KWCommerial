About Robert

Robert Martinez is a certified Commercial and Business Broker with more than 40 years of experience and a proven track record of success, having facilitated the sale of over 40 restaurant and bar concepts. In addition to his extensive work in commercial transactions, he also specializes in residential real estate—helping families find their ideal homes and guiding investors in building strong portfolios across the country.

Bilingual in English and Spanish, he works with clients nationwide to deliver seamless, results-driven real estate solutions. He is a proud member of the National Association of Realtors, the Texas Association of Realtors, and the Houston Association of Realtors, and holds certification as a Short Sale Negotiator. His broad expertise in both commercial and business brokerage enables him to serve a wide range of clients with professionalism, dedication, and integrity.

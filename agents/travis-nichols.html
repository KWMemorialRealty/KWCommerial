About Travis

Travis Nichols is a seasoned commercial real estate professional with KW Commercial Texas in Houston, bringing 24+ years of real estate experience, including 17 years focused on commercial brokerage. His experience spans development tracts, industrial and showroom properties, office and industrial leasing, raw land, and large farm and ranch transactions, including acreage exceeding 1,400 acres. With a broad understanding of land, investment, leasing, and development opportunities, Travis helps property owners, investors, developers, and businesses navigate complex commercial real estate decisions throughout the Houston market and across Texas.

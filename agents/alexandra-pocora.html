About Alexandra

Alexandra is a highly driven and multilingual commercial real estate professional and business brokerage expert, dedicated to delivering a seamless, strategic experience for her clients. With a strong emphasis on building long-term, value-based relationships, she approaches every transaction with focus, energy, and a deep understanding of her clients' goals. Born in Romania, raised in Spain, and having traveled extensively, Alexandra brings a unique global perspective and cultural fluency to every business engagement.

Fluent in Romanian, Spanish (Castilian), and English, she is adept at navigating diverse markets and client needs with ease and professionalism. With a background rooted in entrepreneurship—watching her mother grow a successful business—Alexandra developed an unmatched work ethic, keen negotiation skills, and a sharp eye for opportunity. Her commercial real estate experience spans across working with investors, developers, business owners, and relocating professionals.

She is well-versed in builder relationships, investment analysis, leasing, acquisitions, and business sales. Alexandra has successfully implemented streamlined office systems in high-volume real estate environments and thrives in fast-paced, results-driven settings. She brings expertise in market research, due diligence, contract preparation, CRM platforms, and regulatory compliance on local, state, and national levels.

Whether helping a client buy or sell a business, secure the right commercial space, or explore investment opportunities, Alexandra remains committed to problem-solving, precision, and delivering outcomes that align with her clients' long-term success.

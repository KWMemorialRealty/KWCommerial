About Patrick

Patrick Stuckwish is a Houston-based commercial real estate professional with Keller Williams and KW Commercial, helping business owners, investors, entrepreneurs, and property owners identify opportunities and navigate commercial real estate decisions throughout Greater Houston, across Texas, and through his international network. Patrick's commercial real estate focus includes retail, mixed-use properties, multifamily investments, office, investment properties, and commercial leasing.

Whether helping a business owner find the right location, an investor evaluate an acquisition, or a property owner position an asset for sale or lease, Patrick approaches each transaction with a focus on strategy, market knowledge, communication, and long-term value. His involvement within the real estate industry extends well beyond individual transactions. Patrick serves as a committee member of the Texas REALTORS® Commercial Committee and the Houston Association of REALTORS® Global Committee, and serves on the Board of Directors of the Commercial Real Estate Network (CREN).

He is also a Texas REALTORS® Leadership Program (TRLP) graduate and holds the Certified International Property Specialist (CIPS) designation. Patrick is also actively involved with Sister Cities of Houston and maintains relationships across Houston's business, commercial real estate, and international communities. These connections allow him to bring together property owners, investors, business leaders, real estate professionals, and other resources when opportunities arise.

A United States Marine Corps veteran and longtime entrepreneur, Patrick brings a business owner's perspective to commercial real estate. Having owned and operated multiple businesses himself, he understands that a commercial real estate decision is rarely just about square footage or a building. Location, visibility, demographics, operating costs, financing, customer access, employees, and long-term business goals can all influence whether a property is truly the right fit.

Patrick has built his business around relationships, connections, and problem-solving. His goal is to become a trusted commercial real estate resource clients can call before they need space, when they recognize an investment opportunity, or when they need help determining their needs.

Leadership and recognition: More than 30 years in real estate, title and financial services. He began as a real estate agent with Properties Plus in Las Vegas (1994–1997), then worked in title and financial services with Fidelity National Title, Creditminders (as director of sales, working with mortgage companies), Alamo Title and Chicago Title, rising to President of Fidelity National Title's San Antonio Division (2005–2008). He has led Keller Williams Memorial since 2008, first as managing broker and now as broker/owner. Named 2019 Managing Broker of the Year by Houston Agent Magazine.

About Michael

Michael Bossart is Broker of Record for KW Commercial Texas, the commercial division of KW Memorial in Houston.

He is also the founder of Virtual Realty Assistants, which provides trained virtual assistants to commercial and residential real estate professionals.

About Angelica

Angelica Riveros is a Houston-based Commercial Real Estate Advisor helping business owners, investors, and property owners navigate the acquisition, disposition, and leasing of commercial properties. Her experience includes industrial and warehouse properties, retail, office, commercial land, and investment opportunities. With a strong background in negotiation and a hands-on approach, Angelica provides strategic guidance throughout the transaction—from identifying opportunities and evaluating properties to negotiations, due diligence, and closing.

Fluent in English and Spanish, Angelica is committed to building lasting client relationships through responsiveness, personalized service, and a clear understanding of each client's business and investment goals.

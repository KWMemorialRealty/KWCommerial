About Tim

Director of KW Commercial Real Estate at Keller Williams Memorial – Houston, TX. A seasoned commercial real estate professional, I specialize in the full spectrum of commercial services, including listings, sales, leasing, and portfolio strategies. My expertise spans owner-occupied and investment properties, portfolio acquisitions and dispositions, and distressed asset opportunities—such as short sales, REOs, and value-add repositioning.

With extensive experience across all major asset classes, I bring a strategic, analytical, and results-driven approach to every transaction. I am recognized for my deep understanding of market dynamics, deal structuring, and asset valuation, as well as my unwavering commitment to client success. Whether advising private investors, institutional clients, or business owners, I deliver tailored solutions designed to optimize results and build long-term value.

I hold a BBA in Business Administration/Finance and History from the University of San Diego and have been a licensed real estate professional in Texas since 2017 and in California since 2002. I am also the founder of the CalTex Team, serving clients throughout Texas and Southern California with a collaborative, cross-market advantage. Passionate about excellence and driven by results, I combine local insight with multi-state experience to help clients navigate the complexities of today's commercial real estate market with confidence.

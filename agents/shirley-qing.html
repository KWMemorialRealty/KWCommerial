About Shirley

Shirley Mei Qing resides in Houston, TX. She arrived in the United States in 2000. Shirley graduated with a bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering in China and holds a master's degree in computer science from West Virginia University. Prior to her move, Shirley was employed by Philips Global Lighting Department as a senior sales and marketing representative. Shirley joined Keller Williams in March 2009. With numerous training and continued self-learning she has successfully closed over 15 million dollars in transactions annually.

Shirley founded her real estate team QQ Realty, LLC in 2013. The QQ Realty Team is a bilingual team, fluent in English and Mandarin, experienced in taking care of international clients. Shirley has been serving sellers, buyers and investors in the Memorial, West University, and Sugar Land areas in the Greater Houston area for over 10 years. Shirley combines excellent communication, marketing and negotiation skills with an innate ability to provide exceptional customer service.

This combination allows her to offer outstanding professional service to her diverse list of clients. Her honesty, tireless pursuit of success and dedication to detail has earned her the respect of clients and praise of peers, thus generating a high rate of repeat and referral business. Shirley utilizes the latest technologies, market research and business strategies to exceed expectations. More importantly, she listens and finds solutions tailored to your specific needs.

Shirley and the Team LISTEN to your needs and concerns; DO what has been promised; RESPECT your opinion and choices and SHARE their real estate knowledge and market experience. Shirley has been recognized with the Five Star Real Estate Agent since 2011 by Texas Monthly for excellent customer experience in the Houston real estate market. She is recipient of a Golden Eagle Award for her contribution to the real estate world and community.

Her dedication has provided accomplishments for her Team within the Keller Williams Memorial market center. Shirley and the Team have been recipients of the Keller Williams Memorial Closed Units Individual-3rd Place, 2013; GCI QQ Realty Team 2015-1st place; Keller Williams Memorial 2015 Closed Units- Team QQ Realty; Keller Williams Memorial 2016 Closed Unit Team-2nd Place; Keller Williams Memorial 2018 Closed Units Group – 1st Place; Keller Williams Memorial 2018 Closed Unit Group-3rd Place; Keller Williams Memorial 2017 Closed Unit Group-2nd Place & 4th Place; Keller Williams Memorial 2017 Listing Units Group-2nd Place;.

Shirley also Ranked No. 2 for Closed Volume in July 2019 among 700+ agents in her market center. Finally, Shirley has consecutively earned a spot in the market center's Fantastic Four for having closed a minimum of four transactions per month. Shirley feels blessed and is thankful to her clients and former clients who refer her business and provide the opportunity to serve.
